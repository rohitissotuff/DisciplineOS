# Discipline OS

**Discipline OS** is an offline Android app that scores your day from **0 to 100** based on what you actually did.

Note: This project was primarily generated with AI assistance and is kept as an experiment/reference project. It does not represent my independent development skills.

No accounts. No cloud. No distractions.  
Just your actions, your score, and your consistency.

---

## What this app does

Discipline OS tracks your daily performance using a simple system:

- You follow your workout and habits  
- The app calculates your **Discipline Score**  
- Your **streak continues or breaks** based on that score  

It’s not motivational. It’s honest.

---

## Who this is for

This app is for people who want:

- A **clear daily structure** (workout + habits)  
- A **strict scoring system**  
- **Accountability without distractions**  
- Everything stored **locally on their device**  

If you want reminders, social features, or motivation tricks, this is not that app.

---

## How scoring works

Every day is scored out of **100 points**.

| Category           | Points | How it works                                  |
|--------------------|--------|-----------------------------------------------|
| Workout            | 30     | Based on completed exercises                  |
| Sleep              | 20     | Full score at 8 hours                         |
| Protein            | 20     | Full score at 150g                            |
| Water              | 10     | Full score at 3.0L                            |
| Discipline check   | 20     | Yes/No — did you follow your plan?            |

### Workout scoring

- Training day:  
  `(completed exercises ÷ total exercises) × 30`

- Rest day:  
  Full **30 points** for resting properly

- No plan:  
  Workout score = **0**

---

## Score meaning

| Score   | Meaning        |
|---------|----------------|
| 80–100  | Strong day     |
| 50–79   | Average day    |
| Below 50| Failed day     |

---

## Streak rule

- Your streak continues only if your score is **70 or above**  
- Drop below 70 → streak resets

## App screens

### Today

Your main screen:

- Daily score (color-coded)  
- Workout checklist  
- Habit inputs:  
  - Sleep  
  - Protein  
  - Water  
  - Discipline check  

Update this throughout the day as you complete things.

---

### Plans

Where you create your workouts:

- Import exercises using text  
- Create multiple plans (Push / Pull / Legs, etc.)  
- Assign plans to each weekday  
- Includes a built-in **Rest day**  

---

### Stats

Track your consistency:

- Current streak  
- Weekly average  
- Last 7 days performance (color-coded)  

---

## Getting started

### 1. Add your workout plan

- Go to **Plans**  
- Tap **Import Text**  
- Paste exercises (one per line)  

Example:

```
Bench Press 4x8
Incline DB Press 3x10
Cable Fly
Tricep Pushdown 3x12
```

---

### 2. Use the Today screen

- Check off exercises as you complete them  
- Enter your sleep, protein, and water  
- Mark Discipline check honestly  

Your score updates in real time.

---

### 3. Set your weekly schedule

- Assign different plans to different days  
- Use Rest days when needed  

---

## Daily reset

- Each day is tracked separately  
- At midnight, a new day starts automatically  
- Previous days remain saved for stats and streaks  

---

## Rest days

- Assign a Rest plan to any day  
- You receive full workout points for resting properly  
- Other habits still count  

---

## Data & privacy

- Works fully offline  
- No login required  
- No data is collected or shared  
- All data stays on your device  

If you uninstall the app, your data is deleted.

---

## What this app does not include

- No social features  
- No notifications  
- No cloud sync  
- No calorie tracking or food scanning  
- No advanced workout builder  

This is intentional. The app stays simple so the score stays honest.
